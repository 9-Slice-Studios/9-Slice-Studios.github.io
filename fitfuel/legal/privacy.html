Privacy Policy

Version 2026-11-19 · Effective November 19, 2026

The short version. FitFuel works without an account, and then everything you enter stays on your device: we never receive it. Accounts are optional and only needed to sync your data between devices. If you create one, your data is encrypted on your device before it is stored with our hosting provider, so only you can read it: not the provider, and not us. We do not sell your data or use analytics. The website has no ads. The App Store and Google Play app shows one small banner ad from Google; your food log, workouts and body data are never shared with it (see Ads in the store app).

Who we are

FitFuel is operated by 9-Slice Studios LLC, an Ohio limited liability company ("we", "us"). For anything about privacy, contact hello@9-slicestudios.com.

Using FitFuel without an account

By default FitFuel stores everything in your browser's storage on your own device: your profile (age, sex, height, weight, steps, goals), food log, custom foods and meals, weigh-ins, progress and meal photos, workouts, programs and settings. None of it is sent to us. The food database is a set of files that ships with the app; searching it and looking up a barcode happen on your device, so we do not see what you search for or scan.

Barcode scanning and photos use your camera only when you ask, and only on your device. Camera images are not uploaded. Photos you save stay on the device you took them on.

The app is delivered as a website by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and browser details when your device downloads the app's files, and may keep them in its logs under GitHub's privacy statement. We do not get access to those logs.

FitFuel sets no cookies. It uses your browser's local storage, IndexedDB and offline cache only to make the app work.

You do not need an account to use FitFuel. An account is only for syncing: it lets your phone and computer share the same data. End-to-end encryption. Before anything syncs, FitFuel encrypts it on your device (AES-256) with a key that only your password can unlock. The provider's servers, and anyone who runs FitFuel's database, including us, see only scrambled data, and even the names of items (such as which days you logged food) are hidden. What stays readable to us and the provider is your email address and the technical details in the table above.

If you forget your password. You can always get back into your account with a reset email. Because only your password unlocks your data, nobody can unlock it for you: after a reset, sign in on a device that still has your FitFuel data, and it unlocks your data for your other devices again. Synced data can only be lost if you forget your password and none of your devices still has FitFuel's data; you can then start fresh with what is on your device.

Health information. Food, body-weight and exercise records can reveal things about your health and may count as health data (or "consumer health data") under some laws. We ask for your explicit agreement before you create an account, and we use this data only to provide sync to you.

Email. We use your email address only to run your account: confirming your address, password resets and, rarely, important notices about the service. No marketing.

Supabase and its own infrastructure providers process this data on our behalf under their data processing terms; see Supabase's privacy policy. If you are outside the country where the servers are, your data is transferred to and stored in that country.

Ads in the store app

The FitFuel app you install from the App Store or Google Play shows one banner ad at the bottom of the screen, served by Google AdMob (Google LLC). This is how the app stays free. The website version has no ads.

  • What Google receives. To show and measure the ad and to prevent fraud, Google's ads software in the app collects device and usage information such as your IP address (from which it can estimate a rough location), device and app details, ad views and taps, and diagnostic data. If you allow it (see below), it also uses your device's advertising identifier. Google handles this information as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
  • What Google never receives from FitFuel. Your food log, workouts, body measurements, goals, photos, account or email. FitFuel does not pass any of your data to the ad.
  • Personalized or not. On iPhone, the app asks once whether it may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads are not personalized. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU, the UK and some US states), Google's consent form asks you first, and Settings → Ad privacy choices lets you change your answer.
  • Your controls. iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Android: your phone's Settings → Privacy → Ads. You can also turn off ad personalization in your Google account at My Ad Center.

What we do with your data

  • We use it to keep your devices in sync and to operate, secure and fix the service. Nothing else.
  • We do not sell or rent personal data, and we do not share your FitFuel data (food, workouts, body measurements, account) for advertising. There are no analytics and no third-party scripts. The only third-party code is Google's ads software in the store app, described in Ads in the store app.
  • We disclose data only to the processor named above, or if the law requires it. Google collects its own ad data directly from the store app, as described above.

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on: performing our agreement with you (providing the sync you asked for); your explicit consent for health-related data, which you can withdraw at any time by deleting your account; and our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure.

How long we keep it

Data on your device stays until you delete it, erase the app's data or uninstall. Account data is kept until you delete it or delete your account. When you delete a synced item, its content is removed from the server and a small "deleted" marker (the item's identifier and the time) is kept so your other devices remove it too; markers go when you delete your account. Deleting your account removes your account record and all of your synced data from the live database; copies in the provider's routine backups are overwritten on the provider's schedule.

Your choices and rights

  • See or export your data: Settings → Back up saves everything on the device, in a readable file.
  • Correct it: edit it in the app.
  • Delete it: delete items in the app; or Settings → Delete account to delete your account and all cloud data (see Delete your account). "Erase this device" removes the data from a device.
  • Stop syncing: sign out. The app keeps working on the device.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK, or US states such as California), you may have legal rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the use of your personal data, to receive a copy of it, to withdraw consent, and not to be treated unfairly for using these rights. The tools above cover most of them; for anything else, contact us at hello@9-slicestudios.com and we will answer within the time the law allows. You can also complain to your local data protection authority. We do not "sell" or "share" personal information as those words are used in California law, and we do not use sensitive information for anything other than providing the service.

Children

FitFuel is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. You must be at least 16 to create an account. If you are under 18, use FitFuel with a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Connections to the sync service are encrypted (HTTPS). Synced data is also end-to-end encrypted, as described above, and each account can read and write only its own data, enforced in the database. Your data, your sign-in and your encryption key are stored on your device in readable form, so protect your device with a passcode. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach that affects you, we will tell you as the law requires.

Where the food data comes from

Nutrition data comes mainly from USDA FoodData Central (public domain), plus a built-in list of brand and restaurant items; all of it is included in the app. FitFuel does not contact the USDA or any other third-party service while you use it. See Sources and methods.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the version and date above, and for significant changes we will tell you in the app before they take effect.
