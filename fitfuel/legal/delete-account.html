Delete your FitFuel account

How to delete your FitFuel account and the data stored with it.

In the app (takes effect immediately)

  1. Open FitFuel and tap Settings (the gear in the bottom bar).
  2. Under Account and sync, tap Delete account.
  3. Enter your password and confirm.

This permanently deletes your account (your email address and sign-in) and everything FitFuel stored online for it: your synced food log, weigh-ins, profile and targets, workouts, programs and settings. It cannot be undone.

If you cannot use the app

Email hello@9-slicestudios.com from the address of your account and ask us to delete it. We will delete the account and its data within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is and is not deleted

  • Deleted: your account record and all of your synced data in our database.
  • Kept on your own devices: FitFuel keeps a copy of your data on each device you used, and we cannot reach those. To remove it, tick "Also erase this device" when deleting, or use Settings → Sign out → Erase this device, or clear the site's data / uninstall the app.
  • Backups: our hosting provider's routine database backups are overwritten on its schedule ([OWNER TO FILL IN: how long Supabase backups are kept for your plan, e.g. "within 7 days"]). We do not restore deleted accounts from them.
  • We keep nothing else about you.

Delete only some data

You do not have to delete your account to remove data: delete any food, weigh-in or workout in the app and it is removed from your account on the next sync. To stop syncing but keep using FitFuel on your device, sign out.
