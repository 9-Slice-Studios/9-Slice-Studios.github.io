Health disclaimer

Version 2026-11-18

FitFuel gives estimates for general fitness and information. It is not medical advice, and it is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition.

Talk to a professional first

Talk to a doctor or a registered dietitian before you change how you eat or train, and especially if you:

  • are pregnant or breastfeeding;
  • have a medical condition (for example diabetes, heart, kidney or thyroid disease) or take medication affected by diet or weight;
  • are under 18 or over 65;
  • have, or have ever had, an eating disorder or a difficult relationship with food, weight or exercise.

Everything here is an estimate

  • Calorie needs, "calories burned", expenditure, calorie and macro targets, the thermic effect of food and body-composition projections come from published formulas and averages. Real people differ, sometimes by a lot.
  • Nutrition values come from USDA data and from what you type in. Labels, recipes, brands and portions vary, and data can contain errors. If you have an allergy or a medical diet, always check the actual product label; do not rely on FitFuel.
  • Weight-change timelines are projections, not promises.

Stay safe

  • Very low calorie intakes and very fast weight loss can be harmful. FitFuel's own targets are limited for that reason, but you can type any number: please don't go below what a professional has advised for you.
  • Exercise carries a risk of injury. Use loads and movements that suit your ability, learn safe technique, and stop if you feel pain, dizziness, chest discomfort or shortness of breath. Get medical help if symptoms are serious.
  • FitFuel is not for children under 13. For anyone under 18, FitFuel does not set weight-loss targets; use it with a parent or guardian.

If tracking is making things worse

For some people, counting calories or weighing in often leads to anxiety, guilt or unhealthy habits. If that sounds like you, stop tracking and talk to a doctor or a mental-health professional. In the US you can find support through the National Alliance for Eating Disorders or by calling or texting 988 in a crisis; elsewhere, contact your local health service or eating-disorder charity. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

You use FitFuel at your own risk and are responsible for your decisions. See the Terms of Use for the full legal terms, and Sources and methods for where the numbers come from.
