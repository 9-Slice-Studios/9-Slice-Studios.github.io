How it's figured. Midpoint TEF values from nutrition research: protein 20–30%, alcohol 10–30%, carbs 5–10%, fiber ~5%, fat 0–3%. The range above uses the low and high ends.

Energy per gram: protein 4, carbs 4, fiber 2, fat 9, alcohol 7. Enter total carbs as shown on the label. The calculator subtracts fiber to get net carbs, so fiber isn't counted twice. TEF also varies by person, meal size, and how processed the food is, so treat this as an estimate.
